# Storey

Procedural buildings with walk-in interiors, baked in the editor and drawn at three semantic LODs with a player-aware occlusion system.

- **Manual**: workflows (authoring a building, baking a district, choosing an occlusion mode). _Written per workstream._
- **Reference**: settings (`StoreyQualitySettings`, LOD configuration, occlusion modes). [Quality tiers and the URP settings beside them](quality-tiers.md), with shader warm-up.
- **API**: bake API, `BuildingTable`, `OcclusionSystem`.

Until those pages exist, the design source is `docs/SPEC.md` in the repository, and the port plan is `docs/UNITY-PACKAGE-PLAN.md`.
