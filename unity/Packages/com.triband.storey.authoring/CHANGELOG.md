# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- **Straight flights have a new default look** (docs/EDITOR.md §5): spindles 19 cm apart on a bottom rail, a post after every 4, 14 cm newels on feet, a `#6D6D6D` handrail and `#444445` spindles, posts and newels. *Newel feet* and *Bottom rail* are on until turned off, *Spindles between posts* starts at 4, and the *Colours* card shows the handrail's, spindles' and posts' defaults; *Default* says what it goes back to.
- **Find neighbouring districts links districts alone** (docs/CITY.md §5, docs/PLAY.md §7). A site with **District** off, one placed as a prop, is neither linked nor links others: a prefab moves, and a neighbour is linked by where it stands. Pressed on such a site, it says so and links nothing. The Site settings show **District** with the rest.
- **Shape ▸ Placement lists the outline's corners, each where it stands** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.11): the corners of the outline the Shape tab edits (the Outline card's base or setback), as the Scene view's handles have them, a cut corner one with its cut beside it. Each has its X and Z in metres from the site's origin. A place typed moves it there, one undo step, its cut and its neighbours' following (`CornerCuts.Move`), or says why not. Its number selects it for the Scene view and the Corners card.
- **Shape ▸ Outline makes a circle** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.1): *Circle*, a radius (remembered, 10 m to start), and *Make round*. The outline edited, the base's or a setback's, becomes four corners each rounded as far as it goes (`CornerCuts.Circle`). The inspector says where no wall of it takes a street door, and how to make room for one.
- **Shape ▸ Corners' Radius and Size go as far as the selected corner takes** (`CornerCuts.Largest`), where the slider stopped at 8 m: a circle's corners round all the way, and the slider never pushes a neighbour's cut out. With no corner selected, 8 m as before.
- **A window's or door's triangles walked in** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.9): its inspector says how many it has on a walk-in storey, where the rooms see it from inside too and LOD0 draws the back of what they see behind it (`OpeningKinds.Checked.walkInTris`); *Check on a building* gives the block walked in beside LOD0 and LOD1 (`KindTrial.Result.walkIn`, `plainWalkIn`).
- **The building's outline on every tab** (docs/EDITOR.md §2): the Facade and Interior tabs outline the building being edited as the Shape tool does, in its colour and without its handles: the Facade tab at the outline its style is for, the Interior tab round the storey being edited. The corner, edge and wall handles and the move gizmo stay the Shape tab's (`StoreyTool.TabOutline`).
- **Copy and Paste in place of Copy up** (docs/EDITOR.md §5): the Interior tab's *Floor* card copies the floor's rooms (walls and doors), and *Paste* puts them in place of another floor's. That floor can be in this building, another one, or another site, until the editor closes (script reloads included: the copy is kept in `SessionState`). *Paste* says where its rooms came from and how many walls they have, and each paste is one undo step.
- **The floors are set in the inspector** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.6): the Shape tab's *Storey heights* card is *Floors*, first after *Placement*, with how many floors the building has: typed, or − and + (a new floor copies the top floor's rooms; − takes the top floor off), each one undo step, up to 40. The Interior tab's *Floor* card has *Copy up* (the floor's rooms to every floor above), *Duplicate* and *Delete* for the floor being edited.

### Removed
- **The Problems panel** under the building bar, its *Check* and *Auto*, the background check and its markers in the Scene view (`StoreyProblems`, `StoreyEditView.showProblems`; docs/EDITOR.md §6.5). It wasn't used.
- **Brick patches** in the Facade tab's *Walls* card (docs/EDITOR.md §6.7): the built-in is gone. Add bricks as a facade detail of the project's own, *Scattered on the walls* (§6.12).
- **The Storey Floors overlay** in the Scene view: what it did is in the inspector (above). `StoreyToolContext.Editing`, which only it read, went with it, and the overlay types from the stubs.

### Added
- **Bottom rail, newel feet and height, slim newels and step ends** on selected straight flights (docs/EDITOR.md §5): *Railing* has *Newel height*, *Newel feet* (*Foot width*, *Foot height*) and *Bottom rail* (*Gap under it*, *Width*, *Thickness*), and *Newel width* goes down to slim newels; *Colours* has *Bottom rail* and *Step ends*, each showing what it follows until set.
- **Railing and Colours on selected straight flights** (docs/EDITOR.md §5): *Railing* sets the rails' height, the handrail's width and thickness, the spindles' spacing and thickness, spindles between posts and the posts' width, and the newels' width; *Colours* the handrail, spindles, posts and newels, treads, risers and sides, each with the project's colour picker and *Default*. *Look's defaults* takes them all back. Each change is one undo step.
- **Metal cladding in the Facade tab's Walls card** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.7): *Surface*, *Plain* or *Metal cladding*, with *Panels* (*Vertical* or *Horizontal*), *Panel width*, *Relief* and *Variation*, per style, each change one undo step. The card's summary says which way the cladding runs.
- **Rails and Solid on selected straight flights** (docs/EDITOR.md §5): the Interior tab's *Selected stairs* card has *Rails* (a handrail on balusters up each open side of every flight, and the rails round the floor openings on balusters to match) and *Solid* (each flight solid, filled down to its floor or with a sloped underside over the flight below), both on for new stairs, each change one undo step.
- **Doorway or Door, under the Interior tab's Door tool** (docs/EDITOR.md §5): *Door* sets one of the project's doors, picked under *Door to place*, in a room wall's doorway, facing the side of the wall clicked; the ghost's arrow shows which way. A click on a doorway or a door of another kind makes it the kind picked, and on one of the same kind takes it out, each one undo step. *Doorway* is the prototype's. The inspector notes whether the door picked has an Open prefab. The site keeps the doors placed (`StoreyOpenings.Keep`, and `Sync` on save), so a build has them.
- **Open prefab, on a door's inspector** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.9): set under *LOD1 prefab*. The bake keeps its root's place in the door's own space and its hole's size, and bakes again when it is reimported (`StoreyOpenings.Rebake`).
- **Block, an Interior tool** (docs/EDITOR.md §5): click inside a room to block it out, solid and shut, and click it again to open it, each one undo step. The Scene view outlines the floor's blocked rooms with any tool, and with Block the room a click would block or open. A refused click says why: on a wall, the whole floor (Filled is for that), or stairs or a lift in the room.
- **The Terrace roof card sets a terrace's edge** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.2): when it is a *Terrace*, *Parapet* and *Edge* (*Slab* or *Hanging*, with *Height* or *Hangs down*, *Overhang*, *Top strip*, *Colour* and *Underside*), as the Roof card sets a flat roof's, each change one undo step. The card's summary says *no parapet* and the edge's kind.
- **Enter ends what a Scene view tool is doing** (docs/EDITOR.md §2). In the Interior tab a wall chain ends where it is, as *Finish wall* ends it. A second Enter, or the first with the Door, Erase, Stairs or Lift tool, goes back to Select. In the Facade tab, Enter unpicks *Place doors*, *Place windows*, *Blank wall*, a detail or *Bridge*. It's the editor's one key: `PackageLayoutTests` lets `StoreyTool` read Enter and nothing else, and no other file read a key. The tools' hints and notes say so.
- **A NavMesh Surface's Bake takes in every Storey building** (docs/PLAY.md §5.1, `StoreyNavMeshBake`). While a NavMesh Surface is selected, shown in an Inspector (a locked one too) or baking, every site has every building's collider (`StoreySite.EveryCollider`), so a bake with *Use Geometry* set to Physics Colliders has them all. Before, each bake needed *Colliders in edit mode* turned on by hand on every site first and off again after, and even then it had only the buildings near the Scene view's camera. Nothing to switch, no undo step. Edit mode only. In an assembly of its own (`Triband.Storey.Editor.Navigation`), built only where the AI Navigation package (1.1 or 2.x) is installed.
- **Moving a building moves its Storey Props** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.11, docs/PROPS.md §2.4, `StoreyPropsFollow`): the Shape tool's move gizmo, across and up and down, and *Snap to ground* take the props in the building along by as much as it moves, each prop's transform recorded in the move's own undo step, so one Undo puts them all back. The props in it are those the props manager files under it (`StoreyProp.BuildingId`, the building their inspector names) and, switched off, by where their pivot stands (`PropLocator.BuildingAt`); a prop under another that moves goes with that one. *Its props move with it*, in Shape ▸ Placement, turns it off, kept per user.
- **Place windows** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.7), in the Windows card's *On the walls*, as *Place doors* is in the Doors card: a click on a window in the Scene view takes it out, a click on a bay without one puts one in, on any storey and wall, a blank one's too (`Facades.ToggleWindow`). Where the style has several windows, *Window to place* says which a click puts in: one of its list by name, or *Any of them, by weight*. The Scene view shows the window a click takes out or puts in, by name; the card's summary counts the windows by hand (*· 3 by hand*), and *The style's windows back* drops the tier's. The site's list of windows and doors keeps the windows put in by name.
- **The Facade tab's Preset list shows each preset on the building before it is picked** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.10): while the list is open, the building in the Scene view wears the preset under the pointer, as applying it would make it (its roof kept, its colours matched to the palette). A click applies it, one undo step, and a click outside the list puts the building back as it was. Nothing is written to the layout or the undo history while the pointer moves (`StoreyEdit.Preview`, `EndPreview`): a copy of the building is built, with the neighbours an edit would build again. The list is Storey's own drop-down (`StoreyPreviewMenu`), as Unity's says nothing until a choice is picked: the package's presets, then the project's under *Project* (it was a *Project* submenu), the current one ticked, or *Custom* at the top.

### Fixed
- **Recolour forgot its colour** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.12): turning a detail's *Recolour* off and on again in the Facade tab's *Details* card started again from the detail's own colour. The colour picked is kept while it's off, saved with the style too, and comes back.
- **The Stairs tool places no stairs with nothing to climb to, and says why** (docs/EDITOR.md §5). On the top floor under a pitched roof, switchback stairs came out as a walled stairwell with no steps and no opening between floors, and straight flights as nothing. There, and where filled storeys stop every flight, the ghost is red everywhere. The Scene view and the *Tools* card say why, and so does a click, where it said there was no room.
- **The Interior tools do nothing on a filled storey** (docs/EDITOR.md §5). The tab hides them there, but a tool still picked on another floor went on working: a click with the Stairs tool put stairs on the filled storey.
- **Place windows puts windows in a ground floor with *No windows*** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.7): every click on its bays was refused, saying a door or a lift was there or the storey was too low. A window goes in as the floors above have theirs, over the plinth, and the Scene view's ghost is as big. The *No windows* layout's tooltip says that windows you place stay, as doors do.
- **Place windows puts in the window it names** (docs/EDITOR.md §6.7, `WindowsByHandTests`). With one window in the style, the Windows card showed no *Window to place* and a click put in the style's pick for the bay, while the Scene view said *Put in* that window. The pick could be another: on a ground floor with windows of its own, one of those, bay by bay, and with the style's window weighted 0 beside none, the built-in window. With several windows, the one picked went in, as named. Now a click puts in the window *Window to place* names, by name, on every storey: the style's one window where it has one. Only *Any of them, by weight* puts in the style's pick. On the base, the ground floor's own windows are among the windows to place, so they can still be put in by hand (`Facades.WindowChoices`, `Facades.WindowToPlace`). A click on a window taken out, naming the window the style has there, puts the bay back as before, with nothing kept by hand. A click took its style from the storey over the wall within a centimetre of its tier's top: it takes it from the wall's tier, as the Scene view's label does.
- **Buildings that share an id are told apart on import** (docs/EDITOR.md §2). The importer warns about each building given an id of its own, and opening the layout for editing marks it unsaved, so its next save writes the new ids (`StoreyEdit.Begin`). The importer's version is raised, so Unity reads and bakes every layout again. Before, a duplicate in such a layout left a trail of its LOD0 when dragged.
- Color Pipeline's colour field never makes what it is in wider: the palette colour's name is cut short (all of it in the tooltip), and the suggested colours take as many rows as they need. A field with many suggestions made a narrow inspector scroll sideways.

### Fixed
- Unchecking **Own windows** under the Facade tab's ground floor threw a NullReferenceException (`StoreySiteEditor.SetRows`): the edit lets go of the ground floor's list at once, and the rows were drawn for it from the box's state before the click. They are drawn as the style is now, and `SetRows` draws nothing for a list there isn't.
- A copied Window or Door asset (Ctrl+D, or its file copied in) kept its original's id, so the two were one window: every style that picked either showed whichever loaded last. A copy now gets an id of its own once it's imported, and the original keeps its id, so the styles that picked it still show it (docs/EDITOR.md §6.9). A new window or door takes its asset's GUID as its id; the ids of those made before are kept. Two that already share an id are split when the editor loads, and the Console says which got its own (`StoreyAssetIds`).
- No more console log for every colour matched to the palette while editing (the matching itself is unchanged).
- Making a storey shorter in the Interior tab still flashed its ceiling for a frame. The rebuilt storey was drawn with the floor clip from the frame before. The site now asks the tool for its view when it builds (`StoreySite.ViewSource`), so the clip and the meshes come from the same layout.
- In the Interior tab, going down a floor or making a storey shorter no longer shows the ceiling over the storey for a moment. The clip drops to the new ceiling at once; going up still grows.
- Delete in the Scene view removed the whole Storey Site. With a Storey tool active it now removes the selected wall, stairs, lift, corner or courtyard instead.
- Undoing a deleted Storey Site brought back the layout as last saved, not as edited. The edit is now saved before the site goes, and found again when the site comes back.
- Stairs and lifts are selected by a click, without dragging, and anywhere on them. Dragged past a wall or into another core, they keep sliding along it instead of stopping dead.
- Dragging a wall point or a "+" now moves the walls while dragging. Before, it showed only a dotted preview, and the move could fail to apply on release.
